# Saladin: Listen

An interactive listening app for the audio deep dive **How Saladin Conquered Jerusalem With Mercy**, based on Lauran Paine's book *Saladin: A Man for All Ages* (1974).

The 45-minute episode follows Saladin from watching the failed crusader siege of Damascus as an 11-year-old boy, to taking Egypt without drawing a sword, to the Battle of Hattin, the ransoming of Jerusalem in 1187 and his rivalry with Richard the Lionheart.

**▶ Open it: https://kzaza17.github.io/Saladin/**

## Features

- **24 chapters:** jump straight to any part of the story, from Jerusalem in 1099 to Saladin's death in 1193
- **Transcript mode:** captions light up word by word; tap any line to jump the audio there
- **Reading mode:** a short summary of each chapter, with the matching chapter and pages of Paine's book
- **Illustrations:** historical engravings, woodcuts and manuscript paintings change with the story
- **Full-screen player:** chapter markers, 15-second skips, speeds from 0.75× to 2×
- **Remembers where you were:** your position and saved chapters are kept in your browser
- **Works on phones:** Android and iOS, no app or install needed

## Files

- `index.html`: the whole app in one file, with the audio, illustrations and transcript built in, so it also works offline
- `Saladin_captions.en.srt`: English captions for the episode
